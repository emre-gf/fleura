Keeping the Cost Down

The cheapest appointment is rarely the cheapest outcome — a badly removed set can mean months of repair. A few things genuinely help:

  • Do not miss your refill window. A refill every 2–3 weeks is quicker and cheaper than a brand-new set.
  • Pick the right service. If you only want colour, you do not need extensions.
  • Use cuticle oil and gloves. This visibly extends how long an application lasts.
  • Book as a group. Two or three people at one address lowers the per-person price.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do gel extensions cost in Izmir?

Manicure + gel extensions + gel polish starts from 1.500 ₺. Travel within central Izmir, all equipment and single-use sterile supplies are included. Length, shape and design detail affect the final figure; if you have a set applied elsewhere, removal (500 ₺) is added.

How much is gel polish?

Manicure + gel polish starts from 1.150 ₺. On toes, pedicure + gel polish is 1.250 ₺, and with gel strengthening 1.500 ₺. French, cat-eye or ombre is 250 ₺ extra.

How much is a plain manicure or pedicure?

Manicure starts from 650 ₺ and pedicure from 850 ₺ — shaping, cuticle care and hand or foot treatment, without colour. Both can be booked in the same appointment.

Do you charge extra for travel?

Travel is included in these prices for central Izmir districts. For Çeşme, Alaçatı, Urla and other outlying locations a distance supplement may apply, which we always confirm before the appointment.

Is removing an old set charged separately?

Removal of a set we applied is included in your next appointment. Removing another technician’s work is charged separately: 400 ₺ for gel polish and 500 ₺ for extensions, because it takes extra time. If the product is very thick or damaged the time can increase, and we tell you before starting.

Is there a discount for groups?

When several people at the same address are done one after another we can usually offer a better per-person price. Tell us how many people and which services, and we will quote the total.

How do I pay?

Payment is after the service. Cash and bank transfer are accepted. All prices are in Turkish Lira (₺).
