How It Works

  1. Message us. Send your district or hotel, the service you want and a preferred day on WhatsApp. If you already have gel on, a photo tells us whether removal is needed.
  2. We confirm. We agree a time — usually replying the same day. Weekdays can often be booked 1–2 days ahead, Saturdays 3–4 days ahead; same-day is possible when the schedule allows.
  3. We arrive and set up. Our own work cover, lamp and tools; you sit back at your own table.
  4. We pack up. Aftercare tips, then we clear the area and leave. Payment after the appointment, in cash or by bank transfer.

Home, Hotel, Airbnb or Villa

Most appointments are at clients' homes, but we regularly work in hotel rooms and Airbnbs in Alsancak, Konak and Bayraklı, in residences with concierge access, and in holiday villas on the Çeşme peninsula. For hotels, simply let reception know you are expecting a visitor. For villas in Çeşme, Alaçatı and Urla a travel supplement may apply depending on distance — we tell you before you book, never after.

Groups are welcome: friends, family or a bridal party can be done back-to-back at the same address, with a better per-person rate.

Hygiene

Metal tools are sterilised in an autoclave after every client and carried in sealed pouches. Files, buffers and drill bits are single-use. The work area is disinfected before each appointment and we always work on our own cover. We use professional salon brands such as OPI, CND and Kodi. If a nail shows signs of infection or fungus we do not work on it and recommend seeing a dermatologist first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a mobile nail artist in Izmir who comes to my home or hotel?

Yes. Fleura Nails is a mobile nail service, not a walk-in salon. The nail artist comes to your home, hotel, Airbnb, residence or villa anywhere in Izmir with the lamp, sterilised tools, single-use files and a full colour selection. You only need a table-height surface, two chairs and a power socket.

Can I get gel nails or a gel manicure at home in Izmir?

Yes. Gel polish (kalıcı oje) and gel extensions (protez tırnak) are both done at your address with the same lamp and products a salon uses. Manicure with gel polish takes 45–75 minutes and lasts 2–3 weeks; a first set of gel extensions takes 90–150 minutes.

Do you come to villas in Çeşme and Alaçatı?

Yes. We book appointments at villas, holiday homes and hotels in Çeşme, Alaçatı and Urla. Travel is included in the listed price for central Izmir districts; for the peninsula a travel supplement may apply depending on distance, and we confirm it before booking. In summer, book as early as you can.

Can I call a nail artist to my hotel in Izmir?

Yes. We regularly visit hotels and Airbnbs in Alsancak, Konak and Bayraklı as well as hotels in Çeşme and Alaçatı. Just let reception know you are expecting a visitor; we bring our own work cover and leave the room as we found it.

How much does a mobile manicure cost in Izmir?

Prices are the same as our list prices — travel within central Izmir, equipment and single-use materials are included. Manicure from 650 ₺, manicure with gel polish from 1,150 ₺, pedicure from 850 ₺, manicure with gel extensions and gel polish from 1,500 ₺. Payment after the appointment, in cash or by bank transfer.

Can I book same day, and do you speak English?

Same-day appointments are possible when the schedule allows — message us on WhatsApp and we check availability right away. We reply in English; the website is also available in Turkish and Russian.
