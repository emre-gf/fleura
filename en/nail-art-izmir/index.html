Nail Art in Izmir

A design layer on top of gel polish or extensions — from one fine line to fully hand-painted nails.

Home/Nail Art

Nail art in Izmir, planned with you and applied at your address. Nail art sits on top of an existing application, so we first choose the base — gel polish or extensions — then the design. Fleura Nails is a mobile service, not a walk-in salon: we bring everything to you. All we need is a table-height surface, two chairs and a power socket nearby.

Design Styles

Minimal

Fine lines, single dots, negative space and geometric detail. The most office-appropriate and the most timeless. Adds roughly 15 minutes.

French & Variations

Classic white french, coloured french, reverse french and baby-boomer ombre.

Ombre & Gradients

Two or three colours blended with a sponge or airbrush. Especially striking on longer shapes.

Hand-Painted

Florals, leaves, marble and abstract motifs. Brushwork takes longer per nail, so it is usually kept to accent nails.

Crystals, Foil, Chrome

Crystal stones, gold foil, chrome powder and cat-eye effects — the most requested group for events and evenings out.

Seasonal

Colour palettes and themes that suit the time of year.

How Long Does Nail Art Last?

The design lasts as long as the application underneath it. On gel polish that means 2–3 weeks; on extensions it holds until your refill, roughly 2–4 weeks. A glossy top coat protects the artwork from wear.

What It Goes On Top Of

  • On gel polish — the most common choice, ideal if your nails are already long enough.
  • On gel extensions — gives a larger surface for detailed work, and lets you choose the shape too.
  • As part of bridal nails — planned with a trial run before the wedding day.

Coming to You

Design work is easier when you are relaxed, and we bring everything to your home, hotel or Airbnb. You provide a table-height surface, two chairs and a power socket nearby. Metal tools are sterilised in an autoclave after every client, files and buffers are single-use, and the work area we set up is disinfected before each appointment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you recreate a design I found on Instagram or Pinterest?

Yes, and most clients do exactly that. Send the photo before the appointment so we can check two things: whether it suits your nail length, and how many nails to cover. A design drawn for long stiletto nails looks different on short square ones, and we will explain how to adapt it.

Do my nails need to be long for nail art?

No. Minimal designs — a fine line, a single dot, negative space — look excellent on short nails. Dense detailed artwork needs more surface, so on shorter nails we usually suggest 2–3 accent nails with a clean base.

Does nail art shorten how long the set lasts?

A flat design under a protective top coat does not. Raised elements and crystals catch on things more easily, so if you type a lot or work with your hands, flatter designs are the practical choice.

Can I have art on just a few nails?

That is the most popular option. Two or three accent nails with a plain base looks considered and costs less than covering all ten. Per-nail art is 50–150 ₺ depending on detail — see the price list.
