Premium Mobile Nail Services in Izmir

Professional gel extensions, gel polish, manicure, pedicure and nail art — at your home, hotel or Airbnb. We come to you with sterile, professional equipment.

Looking for nail services in Izmir without hunting for a salon, dealing with traffic or booking by phone in Turkish? Fleura Nails travels to your address. Fleura Nails is a mobile service, not a walk-in salon: we bring everything to you. All we need is a table-height surface, two chairs and a power socket nearby.

What We Do

Gel Nail Extensions

Added length and shape using gel — almond, square, coffin or stiletto. Known locally as “protez tırnak”. Refills every 2–3 weeks.

Gel Polish

Long-wear colour on your natural nails, typically 2–3 weeks of shine. No added length. Sometimes called shellac.

Manicure & Pedicure

Shaping, cuticle care and hand or foot treatment. Available on its own or as the base for colour.

Nail Art

From a single minimal line to hand-painted designs. Send a reference photo and we plan it together.

Bridal Nails

Wedding-day nails with a trial run beforehand, and we come to you on the morning itself.

Prices

Transparent starting prices in Turkish Lira, with travel and sterile supplies included in central Izmir.

Why a Mobile Nail Service Works in Izmir

If you are new to the city, finding a nail technician usually means reading Turkish reviews, calling to book and then crossing town. We remove all three steps. You send a message in English, we agree a time, and the appointment happens where you already are.

  • For residents and expats: no traffic, no parking, no waiting room — useful if you have small children or a full working day.
  • For travellers: we come to your hotel room or Airbnb. Convenient before a wedding, a dinner or a photoshoot.
  • For groups: if several people are at the same address we work through one after another, which usually makes the per-person price more favourable.

Hygiene

Metal tools are sterilised in an autoclave after every client. Files and buffers are single-use and opened in front of you. The work surface is covered with our own protective sheet and disinfected before each appointment.

How Booking Works

  1. Message us on WhatsApp in English with the service you want, your district and your preferred day.
  2. Send a photo of your current nails if you have an existing set — this lets us confirm the duration and whether removal is needed.
  3. We confirm the time and arrive with everything required. Payment is after the service.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have a salon I can visit?

No — Fleura Nails is a mobile service. We travel to your home, hotel room, Airbnb or office anywhere in Izmir and bring all equipment, lamps and single-use supplies with us. There is no physical salon to visit.

Can I book if I do not speak Turkish?

Yes. You can message us in English on WhatsApp and book your appointment in writing. Written English messaging is the smoothest way to confirm the service, address and time.

What do I need to prepare at my address?

Very little: a table-height surface, two chairs and a socket nearby. We lay down our own protective covering, set up the work area and pack everything away afterwards — no dust or residue left behind.

Which areas of Izmir do you cover?

We work across Izmir, including Karşıyaka, Bornova, Alsancak, Balçova, Narlıdere, Buca and Çiğli. Travel is included in the listed prices for central districts; for Çeşme, Alaçatı and Urla a travel supplement may apply, which we always confirm before the appointment.

How do I pay?

Payment is taken after the service. Cash and bank transfer are accepted. Prices are in Turkish Lira (₺) — see the price list.
