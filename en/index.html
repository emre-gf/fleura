Frequently Asked Questions

Things You Ask

Answers to the questions we hear most often

How long does gel polish last?

With correct application and care, gel polish stays glossy and smooth for around 2–3 weeks . The exact time depends on how fast your nails grow and your daily routine. Avoiding long spells in water and wearing gloves for housework both extend it.

Will gel extensions damage my natural nails?

Applied at a balanced thickness and removed properly, gel extensions do not damage your natural nails. Most damage comes from picking or forcing a set off, so removal should always be done by a technician.

How do I book, and is a same-day appointment possible?

Because Fleura Nails comes to your address, every appointment is planned in advance. You can book by phone, WhatsApp or the form on this page, and we agree the day and time together. If our schedule allows we can often fit you in the same day, so do ask — get in touch and we will check availability right away.

What hygiene measures do you take?

Hygiene is our highest priority. All metal tools are sterilised in an autoclave after every client. Files and buffers are single-use, and the work area we set up with our own covering is disinfected before each appointment. We never compromise on this.

Can I have my nails done while pregnant?

During pregnancy, gel polish and manicures are commonly chosen. We would suggest waiting past the first trimester for gel extensions. We can decide what suits you together, and we recommend checking with your doctor as well.

My nails are very brittle — what should I do?

For brittle nails we offer dedicated strengthening treatments . IBX treatment, keratin applications and nourishing masks can all help. At your first appointment we assess your nails and build a care plan around them.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?

We kindly ask that you cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance . That way we can offer the slot to someone else. We understand that last-minute changes happen, but if they become frequent we may ask for a deposit.
