Manicure & Pedicure in Izmir

Hand and foot care at your own address — with sterile tools and single-use files.

Home/Manicure & Pedicure

A manicure or pedicure in Izmir without leaving your home, hotel room or Airbnb. This is the base service — the groundwork that makes colour last — and it can be booked on its own or combined with gel polish. Fleura Nails is a mobile service, not a walk-in salon: we bring everything to you. All we need is a table-height surface, two chairs and a power socket nearby.

What a Manicure Includes

  • Filing and shaping to the length and shape you prefer.
  • Cuticle care — either classic or the dry e-file (Russian) technique.
  • Attention to dry skin and hangnails around the nail.
  • Optional finish with gel polish.

What a Pedicure Includes

  • Toenail shaping and cuticle care.
  • Work on hard skin and calluses on the soles and heels.
  • Care around ingrown-prone edges — tell us in advance if this is a concern.
  • Optional gel polish for colour that survives sandals and the pool.

Classic or Russian (E-File) Manicure?

A classic manicure softens the skin and works with nippers. A Russian manicure is done dry with an e-file, with no soaking and no cutting, and gives a cleaner line right at the base — which is why gel polish applied over it often looks neater for longer. Both are available; tell us which you prefer, or ask and we will recommend one based on your cuticles.

Between Appointments

  • Cuticle oil daily, hand cream after washing.
  • Gloves for cleaning products.
  • File in one direction rather than sawing back and forth.
  • Trim toenails straight across to reduce ingrown edges.

How It Works at Your Address

We bring everything, including a footbath for pedicures. You provide a table-height surface, two chairs and a power socket nearby. Metal tools are sterilised in an autoclave after every client, files and buffers are single-use, and the work area we set up is disinfected before each appointment.

We cover Izmir, including Karşıyaka, Bornova, Alsancak, Balçova, Narlıdere, Buca and Çiğli. Travel is included in the listed price for central districts; for Çeşme, Alaçatı and Urla a travel supplement may apply and we confirm it before booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a manicure and a pedicure?

A manicure covers the hands and fingernails; a pedicure covers the feet and toenails. Both include shaping and cuticle care, but a pedicure also addresses hard skin on the soles and heels.

Can I have both in one appointment?

Yes, and most clients do. It takes longer than a single service, which we account for when scheduling. Booking both at once saves a second visit.

What is a Russian manicure?

It is a dry technique worked with an e-file rather than soaking and cutting, giving a very clean result close to the cuticle. It is popular with clients who dislike cuticle nippers and want gel polish to sit closer to the base.

Can I have a pedicure while pregnant?

Pedicures are commonly chosen during pregnancy, and having one at home helps in the later months when bending becomes difficult. We work with low-odour products in a well-ventilated space, and we recommend checking with your doctor first.

How often should I book?

Roughly every 3–4 weeks for a manicure and every 4–6 weeks for a pedicure, though it depends on your nail growth and daily routine.
