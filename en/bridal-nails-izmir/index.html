Bridal Nails in Izmir

A trial run beforehand, and on the wedding morning we come to you.

Home/Bridal Nails

Bridal nails in Izmir planned around your dress and your day. Because we work at your address, nobody has to travel separately on the morning itself. Fleura Nails is a mobile service, not a walk-in salon: we bring everything to you. All we need is a table-height surface, two chairs and a power socket nearby.

How We Plan It

  1. Talk it through: send your date, your dress style and any reference images.
  2. Trial run: we test shape, length and shade on your own hands and adjust.
  3. The day: we come to your address and apply the final set.

Choosing Shape and Colour

Bridal designs tend to work best when they photograph cleanly: soft nudes, classic french, baby-boomer ombre and fine detail hold up better in close-up photos than very busy artwork. Length matters too — if you are not used to long nails, the wedding day is not the moment to try them for the first time, which is exactly what the trial run is for.

For the Bridal Party

Mothers, sisters and friends can be booked into the same visit. Group appointments at one address are usually more favourable per person, and they remove the logistics of everyone finding their own appointment.

Related Services

  • Gel extensions if you want added length for the day.
  • Gel polish for a clean, long-wearing colour.
  • Nail art for fine detail and accents.

We cover Izmir, including Karşıyaka, Bornova, Alsancak, Balçova, Narlıdere, Buca and Çiğli. Travel is included in the listed price for central districts; for Çeşme, Alaçatı and Urla a travel supplement may apply and we confirm it before booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

Wedding dates fill up quickly in season, so as early as you can. We usually plan a trial run a few weeks before and the final application close to the day itself.

Why is a trial run useful?

It lets you see the shape, length and colour on your own hands, check it against your dress and change anything before the day. It also tells us exactly how long the final appointment will take.

Can you do the bridal party as well?

Yes. If several people are at the same address we work through one after another, which is far easier on the morning than everyone travelling separately. Tell us how many people so we can allow enough time.

Do you come on the wedding morning?

That is the usual arrangement — we come to you, so nails are done where you are already getting ready. Confirm the address and timing with us when booking.
